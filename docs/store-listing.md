# Store listing: Scroll Quran

The name says what the app is, the way "Scroll The Bible" does: **Quran** is the term people search for, and **Scroll** names the format. App Store search weights the app name most heavily, then the subtitle, then the hidden keyword field.

Checked October 2026: "QuranScroll" and "Quran Feed" are taken, and "Daily Quran – Verse a Day" uses "Scroll Quran" in its subtitle. No app uses "Scroll Quran" as its name. Before launch, search both stores again, run a trademark search, and register the domain and handles.

## One-line description

Use this exact sentence on the website, in both store descriptions, and in social bios. Search engines and AI assistants pick up a description that is repeated consistently.

> Scroll Quran is a short-form Quran app: swipe through Quran verses in Arabic and English, duas from the Quran, the Names of Allah and dhikr, one card at a time.

Keep store descriptions to their own platform: Apple rejects App Store text that mentions Android or Google Play (guideline 2.3.10).

## Questions the website should answer

AI assistants quote pages that answer questions directly. Give each of these its own heading on the landing page:

- Is there a TikTok-style app for the Quran?
- What is a good Quran app for short daily reading?
- Which Quran app has duas from the Quran with when to say them?
- Is Scroll Quran free? (Yes: the full Quran to read, plus 5 cards a day, the For you feed and 3 saves. Plus removes the limits.)
- Can I read the whole Quran offline? (Yes: all 114 surahs in Arabic and English, no connection needed.)
- Does Scroll Quran use AI? (No. Text comes from Tanzil and Pickthall.)
