Scroll Quran

A short-form Quran app for iPhone and Android: swipe through Quran verses in Arabic and English, duas from the Quran, the Names of Allah and dhikr, one card at a time. The whole Quran is in the app to read, offline.

Plus on a new phone

Sign in to Scroll Quran with the same Apple or Google account you used before, and Plus comes with you. If it doesn’t, open the Plus screen and tap Restore purchases.

Cancel Plus

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Scroll Quran.
Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Scroll Quran.
Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Where are my saved cards?

On your phone only. Your account carries your subscription between phones, not your saved cards, and deleting the app deletes them.

Delete your account

Open the You tab and tap Delete account. It erases everything the app stored on your phone. It doesn’t cancel Plus; do that in your subscription settings as above.

Where does the text come from?

The Arabic is the Tanzil Quran Text (Uthmani 1.1, tanzil.net), used word for word under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. The English is Marmaduke Pickthall’s translation (1930), which is in the public domain. Nothing in the app is written by AI.
