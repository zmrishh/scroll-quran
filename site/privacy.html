Privacy Policy

Scroll Quran for iPhone and Android · Effective 28 October 2026

Scroll Quran has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your saved and liked cards, the reflections you write, your mood check-ins, your reading place in the Quran, your streak and your settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Your phone may include this information in its own backups (iCloud or Google). Those backups are covered by Apple’s or Google’s privacy policies, not ours.

Subscriptions

Scroll Quran Plus is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google takes the payment; we never see your name, email address or card details.

To know whether Plus is active, and to restore it on a new phone, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. Each time the app opens, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random ID the app creates, which is not linked to your name or email address;
  • your Scroll Quran purchases and subscription status, if you have any;
  • technical details: device type, operating system and app version, the country of your store account, and your IP address.

This is used only to unlock Plus and keep it working. It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold.

Sharing cards

When you share a card, the image is made on your phone and handed straight to the app you pick. We don’t receive it.

Links

Links in the app, such as to the Tanzil Project, open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Scroll Quran does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • To remove everything on your phone, delete the app.
  • To cancel Plus, use your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • To have your subscription record at RevenueCat deleted, email us with your App Store or Google Play order ID and we will delete the matching record.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
